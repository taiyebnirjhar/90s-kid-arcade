KOF Arcade

Neo Geo fighters with one-tap supers.

    Add your game zips (for example kof97.zip, kof2002.zip, kf2k2mp.zip) and the Neo Geo BIOS neogeo.zip. Pick them all at once.

    Files stay on this device. Only use ROMs you own.

    How the controls work
    • Left half: put your thumb anywhere, the stick appears under it.
    • A B C D: hold, or slide between them for combos.
    • Gold buttons: one tap does the whole move, e.g. Iori's Ya Otome.
    • Facing: moves are written for P1 on the left. When your fighter swaps sides, tap the facing pill at the top.
    • ⚙: pick a character's moves or write your own (numpad notation like 2363214+A).
    • Keyboard: arrows/WASD, J K U I = A B C D, 1–6 moves, F flip, Enter start, Backspace coin.
