What it does

Built for each game, not a generic gamepad

An on-screen controller that knows the game you're playing, the fighter you picked and which way they face.

Fighters

Specials in one tap

Every super and special of your fighter on a ring around the buttons, drawn as its stick motion. One tap plays the whole input, frame-exact.

Automatic

Knows who you're fighting with

Reads the game to see your team, who's on and which way they face, so the ring always matches. Follows tag teams in Marvel vs Capcom and your Super Art in Street Fighter III.

Checked

Every move tested

Each move was played in the real games exactly as the app plays it, and kept only if the fighter really did it.

Shooters

Rapid fire

Hold Shoot in Metal Slug or Fire in Nightmare in the Dark to keep firing, at the rate that fires the most.

Yours

Your layout

Floating or fixed stick, size, opacity, left-handed, vibration. Drag any button where your thumbs want it.

Easy

Any file name

Pick a game's zip from your phone: the app works out which game and version it is, and says exactly what's missing if anything is.

Play together

One phone per player.
No internet needed.

Turn on one phone's hotspot and connect the others (or share a Wi-Fi). The phone with the hotspot can play too.

  1. Open a roomPick the game and it starts. You get a room key like K7Q-2M, with a Copy button.
  2. Friends join with the keyOr tap your room in the list of rooms nearby. Nobody has to let them in: they come in watching the match.
  3. COIN, then START to playTakes a free seat (in KOF: "Here comes a new challenger!"). A player idle for 20 seconds gives up their seat to the next one. First to press gets it.
  4. Leave any timeThe game goes on for everyone else, even if the host leaves. Up to 8 phones in a room.

Built like online fighting games (rollback netcode): each phone runs the game and only button presses travel, so it feels like playing on one machine. Watchers never slow the players down.

Install in a minute

  1. Download it on your phoneTap Download for Android: 90s-Kid-Arcade.apk downloads straight away.
  2. Open the downloaded fileAndroid asks to allow installing apps from your browser the first time: allow it, then Install. New versions install over the old one and keep your games and settings.
  3. Add the Neo Geo BIOSOn the home screen, tap Add BIOS and pick your neogeo.zip (once; the Capcom games don't need it).
  4. Tap a gameThe first time, pick its zip from your phone (any file name), or download it from your own library. Then COIN, START, and play.

Questions

FAQ

Are the games included?

No. The app comes with no games or BIOS: use dumps of games you own. It checks each zip and tells you exactly which files are missing, if any.

Does multiplayer use mobile data?

No. Everything travels over the hotspot or Wi-Fi between the phones. The hotspot phone doesn't even need a SIM with data.

How many can play?

As many as the game takes: 2 in most, 3 in Cadillacs and Dinosaurs. Up to 8 phones can be in a room; the rest watch and can take a seat with COIN or START.

Do all phones need the same version?

Yes: every phone needs the latest app and the same game files. The app tells you if they differ.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8 or newer. Neo Geo games run on almost any phone; Street Fighter III and Marvel vs Capcom like a recent one.

Is it open source?

Yes, under GPL-3.0, on GitHub. It runs the FBNeo arcade emulator through LibretroDroid.
