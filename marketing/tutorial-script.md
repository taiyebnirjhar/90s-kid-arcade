# App tutorial video: script

What it is: a tutorial people actually watch: a hook first, then the app
in the order a new player meets it, then the fun part. Real app screens
only (screen recordings), with captions and a voice-over in the owner's
voice: short, plain, no hype.

Two cuts from the same recordings:
- **Reel** (vertical 1080x1920, about 75 s): Facebook Reels, Shorts, TikTok.
- **YouTube** (horizontal, about 3 min): the same scenes, a little slower,
  each step shown in full.

## Before recording

- **Show taps:** Settings → Developer options → **Show taps**, so viewers
  see every touch. (Developer options: tap Build number 7 times.)
- **Phone clean:** Do Not Disturb on, full battery icon, Wi-Fi on.
- **Screen recorder:** with **media sound** on (game sound), mic off (the
  voice-over is recorded separately, in a quiet room).
- **A friend's phone** ready for the online part (a room, chat, voice).
- **Library key:** never show it on screen; blur or skip that moment.

## Script (Reel, about 75 s)

| # | Time | Record (real screen) | Caption on screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-3 s | **HOOK.** In a KOF fight, your thumb taps one button and Rugal's super comes out, full screen. | **KOF on your phone. Supers in one tap.** | "This is KOF on your phone. One tap, super move." |
| 2 | 3-7 s | Home screen: slow scroll through the game rows (KOF, Metal Slug, Capcom). | **30 arcade classics** | "All the arcade games we grew up with. Free." |
| 3 | 7-15 s | **INSTALL.** The website on the phone → **Download** → open the file → **Install** (allow once if Android asks). | **Download free:** arcade.taiyebnirjhor.com | "Download from our site, open it, install. Takes a minute." |
| 4 | 15-22 s | The welcome screens: **"Taiyeb Nirjhor invited you"** → the BIOS step → "Save your matches?" | **First time? It walks you through.** | "First time, the app shows you everything step by step." |
| 5 | 22-30 s | **GAMES.** Home → **Library** → a game downloading (progress bar) → it shows **Play**. | **Add your games, tap Play** | "Add your games, and tap play." |
| 6 | 30-42 s | **PLAYING.** A KOF fight. Show the stick and A B C D, then the **move buttons with names**; tap one, the special comes out; tap the super. | **Every special has its own button** | "The stick and four buttons, like the arcade. And every special and super has its own button. No more missing the move." |
| 7 | 42-45 s | Pause menu: **Save**, **Load**, **Controls** (button size). | **Save anywhere · resize the buttons** | "Save anywhere, make the buttons as big as you want." |
| 8 | 45-55 s | **ONLINE.** **Online** → **Make a room** → the room code / QR → the friend's phone joins (show both phones side by side if possible). | **Play friends online** | "Want to play your friend? Make a room, send the code. They're in." |
| 9 | 55-62 s | In the fight: **chat bubbles** come in over the game; tap the **mic** and talk; the **speaker** button to mute them. | **Chat and voice in the room** | "Chat and talk while you fight. Too much trash talk? Mute." |
| 10 | 62-67 s | **Friends** list: green dot online → **Challenge** → the friend gets it. | **Challenge friends in one tap** | "Add friends, see who's online, challenge them." |
| 11 | 67-72 s | **Replays** → the match → the **download** icon → "Saving the video…" → **Share it** → WhatsApp. | **Save your best match as a video** | "Won a good one? Save it as a video, send it to the group." |
| 12 | 72-76 s | The end card (logo, features, the address). | **Free on Android · arcade.taiyebnirjhor.com** | "90s Kid Arcade. Free. Link in bio." |

**Music:** a low retro beat under the voice, game sound up in scenes 1, 6
and 9. **Pace:** a cut every 2-3 seconds; zoom in on the tap in scenes 1
and 6 so the one-tap super is the clearest thing in the video.

## YouTube cut (about 3 min)

The same 12 scenes, longer:
- Scene 3 shows the full install, including "allow installing apps" once.
- Scene 5 shows the BIOS first ("every Neo Geo game needs it").
- Scene 6 shows two games: KOF (move buttons) and Metal Slug (rapid fire).
- Scene 8 shows open rooms (anyone can join) and locked rooms (code only),
  and **Quick match**.
- Chapters in the description: Install 0:20, Games 0:50, Controls 1:10,
  Online 1:50, Friends 2:20, Replays 2:35.
