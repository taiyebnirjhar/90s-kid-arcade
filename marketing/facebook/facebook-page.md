## Longer description (About › Details)
Grew up playing KOF and Metal Slug in arcade shops after school. Wanted the
same thing on phone, with friends. So I built it.

90s Kid Arcade runs the old arcade games on Android. Specials are one tap,
there is rapid fire too. You can make a room and play friends online from
anywhere, with chat and voice. No internet? Turn on one phone's hotspot and
play nearby.

Free. No ads. Nothing to buy.

Download: https://arcade.taiyebnirjhor.com
